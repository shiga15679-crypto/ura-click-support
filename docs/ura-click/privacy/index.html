Ura Click - Offbeat Metronome

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ura Click - Offbeat Metronome handles information when you use the app. The app is developed by Shoichi Hasegawa.

Information We Collect

Ura Click does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or contact details inside the app.

The app may process app settings and practice preferences on your device, such as BPM, time signature, bar settings, mode selection, and saved presets. These settings are used to operate the app.

Third-party services used by the app may collect or process certain technical information, such as device identifiers, purchase status, product identifiers, advertising identifiers, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and similar information as needed to provide their services.

How We Use Information

  • To provide metronome playback and practice features.
  • To save app settings and presets on the device.
  • To verify Pro purchase status and restore purchases.
  • To display ads to non-Pro users.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve app functionality.

Advertising

Ura Click uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and interstitial ads to users who have not purchased the Pro version. Google AdMob may use IP addresses (which may be used to estimate approximate location), device identifiers, advertising identifiers, ad interaction data, and related technical information to provide and measure ads.

Ads are not shown when the Pro entitlement is active.

In-App Purchases

Ura Click offers a one-time, non-consumable Pro purchase. The product ID is pro_lifetime, and the Pro entitlement is pro.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store on iOS and by Google through Google Play on Android. RevenueCat is used to manage purchase status, entitlement status, and purchase restoration. The developer does not receive your full payment card details.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Expo / React Native runtime libraries used to build the app.

Data Management

App settings stored on your device can generally be removed by deleting the app from your device. Purchase history is managed by Apple or Google Play, depending on the platform, and purchase entitlement status is managed through RevenueCat. You can restore purchases from within the app.

If you contact support by email, the information you provide in that email will be used only to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

Children's Privacy

Ura Click is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support inquiry, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

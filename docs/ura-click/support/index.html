Ura Click - Offbeat Metronome

Ura Click is a metronome practice app designed to help musicians work on steady timing, frontbeat clicks, offbeat clicks, time signatures, BPM changes, and bar-based switching exercises.

FAQ

What is the Pro version?

The Pro version is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It removes ads from the app and enables Pro features such as preset saving where available.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Pro area in the app and tap the restore button. Please use the same Apple ID that was used for the original purchase.

How can I remove ads?

Ads are removed after the Pro version is purchased and the Pro entitlement is active. Pro users should not see banner ads or interstitial ads.

What should I do if there is no sound?

  • Check the device volume.
  • Check Silent Mode and Focus settings on the device.
  • Try stopping and starting playback again.
  • Restart the app if the audio session does not recover.

How can I contact support?

Send an email to sho15679@gmail.com. Please include the app name, your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue.
